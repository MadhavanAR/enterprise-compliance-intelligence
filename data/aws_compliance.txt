AWS Compliance Programs

Amazon Web Services (AWS) maintains compliance programs that help customers
satisfy regulatory requirements such as SOC 1, SOC 2, SOC 3, ISO 27001,
ISO 27017, ISO 27018, GDPR, and HIPAA.

AWS provides customers with access to compliance reports through AWS Artifact.
These reports include independent audit attestations and certifications.

AWS complies with global data protection laws and offers Data Processing
Addendums (DPA) to meet GDPR obligations.
